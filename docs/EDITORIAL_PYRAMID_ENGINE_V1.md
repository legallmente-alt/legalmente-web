# LegalMente — Editorial Pyramid Engine V1

Status: executable repository rule.

## Purpose

Prevent repetitive, unattractive or prematurely specialized content while preserving LegalMente's pan-Hispanic legal rigor and visual identity.

## Depth progression

The content engine should normally progress from foundations to specialization:

1. FOUNDATIONS — universal legal ideas, justice, rule, responsibility, good faith, consent, evidence.
2. PERSON_AND_DIGNITY — person, dignity, equality, liberty and human-rights foundations.
3. RIGHTS_AND_DUTIES — rights, duties, obligations and basic legal relationships.
4. CORE_INSTITUTIONS — contract, property, possession, representation, liability, evidence, breach and reparation.
5. LEGAL_BRANCHES — civil, labor, family, corporate, commercial, administrative, tax, agrarian, etc.
6. CONCRETE_PROBLEMS — recognizable practical conflict patterns.
7. SPECIALTIES — deeper domain and professional treatment.
8. JURISDICTION_AND_PROCEDURE — country, authority, procedure, case law and time-sensitive positive law.

This is Kelsen-inspired as a depth architecture only. It must not be presented as a universal doctrinal hierarchy of positive-law sources. Natural-law and human-rights ideas may be used as conceptual foundations without falsely claiming that every jurisdiction ranks them identically.

## Mandatory rules

- Basic-first by default. Do not rush into country-specific procedure while useful foundational coverage is still thin.
- Pan-Hispanic neutral by default. Explicit jurisdiction is mandatory at jurisdiction/procedure depth and whenever a legal proposition depends on a specific legal system.
- Utility over encyclopedic completeness. Reject low-value filler.
- Anti-repetition is semantic, not title-based. Compare topic + angle + legal relationship + audience + format + visual metaphor against history.
- A batch of exactly 10 pieces must use at least 7 editorial formats and at least 4 visual grammars.
- Adjacent pieces may not repeat both editorial format and visual grammar.
- Keep existing source, evidence, privacy, human-review and Founder/publication gates. This engine chooses and diversifies content; it does not waive legal QA.

## Editorial formats

MEMORABLE_PHRASE, REFLECTION, CONCEPT, GUIDE, STEPS, CHECKLIST, COMPARISON, LEGAL_MYTH, HUMAN_SCENE, HISTORY_CULTURE, QUESTION, ELEGANT_INSTITUTIONAL.

## Visual grammars

CINEMATIC_PHOTOGRAPHY, EDITORIAL_STILL_LIFE, CLASSICAL_REINTERPRETATION, ARCHITECTURAL_MINIMALISM, HISTORICAL_DOCUMENTARY, CONCEPTUAL_SYMBOLISM.

## Batch behavior

A ten-image request is a mini editorial collection, not ten clones. Vary editorial function, composition, text density, protagonist object/scene, scale, rhythm, artistic treatment and narrative structure while preserving LegalMente's authority, elegance, clarity and mobile legibility.
