# LegalMente Knowledge Opportunity Engine V1

## Purpose

This layer joins two complementary systems without mixing their authority:

1. **Knowledge / legal architecture** decides what a legal unit is, how it relates, which jurisdiction/date/evidence matters, and whether canonical legal authority is available.
2. **Opportunity / gap architecture** decides what LegalMente should investigate, explain, package or distribute next based on audience value, journey gaps, strategic fit and evidence of demand.

The opportunity engine never approves legal claims and never opens a legal gate.

## Core distinction

A topic can be strategically attractive and still be legally unready.

```text
OPPORTUNITY SIGNAL
  -> knowledge gap
  -> audience / journey / product value
  -> prioritization hypothesis
  -> research / canonical binding
  -> legal gate
  -> editorial adaptation
  -> distribution
  -> measured outcome
  -> new opportunity evidence
```

Demand evidence and legal authority are orthogonal dimensions.

## Why the two-agent synthesis is stronger

The architecture report correctly defines LegalMente as a transformation engine:

`human question -> context -> concept -> normative context -> source -> claim -> relation -> comprehension route -> preparation -> next action`

The gap-analysis report correctly identifies a second problem: even a good knowledge graph needs to know which missing routes, clusters, tools or distinctions are worth building first.

V1 therefore adds an explicit Opportunity Engine between signals and production.

## Evidence discipline

Opportunity prioritization supports four evidence classes:

- `EDITORIAL_HYPOTHESIS`
- `INTERNAL_QUALITATIVE`
- `EXTERNAL_RESEARCH`
- `MEASURED_FIRST_PARTY`

Only measured first-party evidence may be represented as an observed metric in the engine.

A qualitative internal recommendation must not be rewritten as search volume, SEO difficulty, ranking, traffic or conversion data.

This is especially important for the 2026-09-03 gap-analysis demonstration: its real-estate and contract priorities are useful hypotheses, but the source explicitly states that no Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush ranking export was available.

## Gap types

The engine classifies missing value as:

- `FOUNDATIONAL_GAP`: a prerequisite concept or basic route is missing.
- `DEPTH_GAP`: a topic exists but lacks the next meaningful layer.
- `RELATION_GAP`: concepts exist but their connection is not visible or reusable.
- `JOURNEY_GAP`: the user has no clear next step between awareness, understanding, preparation and human handoff.
- `PRODUCT_GAP`: reusable knowledge exists but is not packaged into a bounded tool/checklist/workflow.
- `DISTRIBUTION_GAP`: knowledge exists but lacks the right audience adaptation or surface.
- `FORMAT_GAP`: the idea is known but not expressed in a useful editorial/visual form.
- `FRESHNESS_GAP`: an existing route needs re-verification because law, context or signal changed.

## Strategic pillars

Every opportunity belongs to one planning pillar:

- `FOUNDATIONAL_KNOWLEDGE`
- `MASS_PUBLIC_VALUE`
- `PROFESSIONAL_AUTHORITY`
- `PRODUCT_PREPARATION`
- `CURRENT_SIGNAL`

This prevents the whole roadmap from being captured by whichever cluster was most recently researched.

## Portfolio rule

LegalMente should not choose the next ten pieces merely by descending score.

`selectOpportunityPortfolio` balances:

- opportunity score;
- evidence confidence;
- cluster concentration;
- strategic-pillar coverage;
- public/professional audience coverage.

Default concentration is capped so a single cluster cannot dominate the whole batch. This rule is deliberately stronger than the Manus demonstration recommendation: real-estate may be a strong pilot cluster, but it is not allowed to become the permanent ontology or content monopoly.

## Score semantics

The score estimates **what to investigate/build next**, not legal correctness.

Inputs:

- impact;
- strategic fit;
- knowledge gap;
- ease to win;
- professional/product value;
- source readiness;
- effort;
- jurisdiction sensitivity;
- confidence of opportunity evidence.

Low source readiness does not erase a strong opportunity. It routes the item toward research before production.

High jurisdiction sensitivity does not erase a strong opportunity. It forces territory-aware validation before conclusive output.

## Seed opportunities from the two-agent review

V1 encodes eight hypotheses, including:

- pre-signing preparation hub;
- representation / authority checklist;
- distinction between subdividing, urbanizing and formalizing title;
- real-estate development architecture;
- due-diligence baseline and findings matrix;
- LegalMente scope / trust / professional handoff page;
- reconciliation of marketing, contract and operational reality;
- permission-dependency matrix as a territory-parametrized tool.

These records are planning inputs only. They are not canonical legal claims.

## Architecture after synthesis

```text
HUMAN / MARKET / EDITORIAL SIGNALS
        |
        v
OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
  gap + audience + journey + value + evidence confidence
        |
        v
KNOWLEDGE GRAPH / DEPTH ENGINE
  concepts + relations + depth + anti-repetition
        |
        v
PSYCHE CANON
  source + claim + jurisdiction + version + legal gate
        |
        v
NEXT-ACTION ENGINE
  stop / ask / learn / prepare / explore / adapt
        |
        v
EDITORIAL ROUTER
  public / founder LinkedIn / radar / product preparation
        |
        v
SURFACE
  LegalMente public / web knowledge / LinkedIn / product tool
        |
        v
OUTCOME MEMORY
  measured engagement + qualified questions + tool use + professional conversations
        |
        +------> opportunity evidence for the next cycle
```

## Non-goals

V1 does not:

- claim measured SEO demand where none was supplied;
- turn LinkedIn popularity into legal truth;
- create a second claim/source registry in `legalmente-web`;
- make real-estate the universal center of LegalMente;
- publish automatically;
- deploy automatically;
- replace Psyche's legal authority;
- infer a universal legal hierarchy from Kelsen.

## Next expansion

After this engine is stable, the most valuable next layer is persistent outcome memory with separate measurements for:

- public reach;
- qualified engagement;
- learning completion;
- checklist/tool use;
- professional authority conversations;
- product intent;
- source readiness time;
- content repetition avoided.

Those measurements should update opportunity evidence without changing canonical legal authority.
